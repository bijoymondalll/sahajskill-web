# Product Overview: SahajSkill (VocaLearn AI)
SahajSkill is India's first hyperlocal, AI-powered study & career platform built specifically for Class 11 & Class 12 Vocational Engineering & IT students under the WBSCTVESD (West Bengal State Council of Technical and Vocational Education and Skill Development) board.

## Core Philosophy
1. **100% Dynamic Admin-Driven CMS:** Nothing is hardcoded. Classes (Class 11, Class 12, VOCLET), statuses (ACTIVE vs COMING_SOON), Trades/Subjects (ETIT, ETEM, ETCC, EDCA, Physics, Chemistry, Math), Study Notes, Exam Suggestions, Mock Tests, Live Classes, AI Prompts, and Force Update flags are 100% controlled via the `/admin` dashboard.
2. **MacOS Liquid Glass UI:** Ultra-modern dark/glassmorphic interface (`backdrop-blur-2xl bg-white/10 border-white/20`), smooth Framer Motion transitions, and a floating MacOS-style dock navigation.
3. **Bilingual Accessibility:** Supports Bengali + English friendly study workflows tailored for rural and semi-urban students.
